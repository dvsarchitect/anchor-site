**"Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path." — Psalm 119:105**

We are in the fog of war.

The world is loud. The culture is screaming. The pressure to conform, to capitulate, to numb yourself with comfort is a constant artillery barrage.

In the chaos, you do not need more noise. You need a map.

When a soldier is dropped into hostile territory, he doesn't guess his azimuth. He checks his compass. He consults his map. He relies on his training.

For too long, we have been trying to navigate this cultural minefield on instinct. We've been "winging it."

That ends today.
